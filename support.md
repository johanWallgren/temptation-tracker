# Temptation Tracker – Support

**Questions, problems or ideas?**
[Open an issue](https://github.com/johanWallgren/temptation-tracker/issues/new) (needs a free GitHub account).
Issues are public, so please don't include personal details.

## Common questions

**Where is my data?**
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Settings → Full version → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

**What happens when the free trial ends?**
You can still see your history, summaries and plans, change settings and export your data.
Logging new temptations and check-ins needs the one-time full version.

**How do I get my data out?**
Settings → Data → Export (CSV files).

**Why doesn't the app suggest a place?**
It needs location access (Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Temptation Tracker)
and a saved place nearby.
