# Temptation Tracker – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

Temptation Tracker is made by Johan Wållgren, an individual developer in Sweden.
This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

## The short version

Everything you log stays on your iPhone. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and
no tracking, and it doesn't send your data to me or to anyone else.

## What the app stores

The app stores what you enter, only on your device:

- temptations you log: type, time, outcome, intensity, place, people and activities
- daily check-ins: your rating of the day, estimates and day factors
- your temptation types, saved places, companions, activities and if–then plans
- your settings, such as reminder times

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS. It is
included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) the same way as other app data. The
app does not sync it to any server.

## Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location only when you log a temptation, to suggest
the nearest of your saved places. Your current location is not saved with your entries
and never leaves your device. When you save a place, its coordinates are stored on your
device so it can be suggested later. You can deny or change location access at any time
in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

## Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders, "Still tempted?" notices and Live Activities are scheduled on your device by
iOS. Their content isn't sent to me.

## Purchases

The free trial and the full version are handled by Apple through the App Store. I don't
receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to
purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Exporting and deleting your data

You can export your data as CSV files (Settings → Data). Where the files go is up to you.

You can delete single entries and check-ins in the app. Deleting the app removes all its
data from your iPhone (but not from backups you made earlier).

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
